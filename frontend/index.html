QUESTION Show me the top 5 customers by total spending.
↓
CONTEXT RETRIEVED customers, orders, order_items
↓
GENERATED SQL 
SELECT c.name, SUM(o.total) AS spending
FROM customers c
JOIN orders o ON c.id = o.customer_id
GROUP BY c.name
ORDER BY spending DESC
LIMIT 5;
